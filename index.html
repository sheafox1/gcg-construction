How It Works

How a project moves with GCG

From first call to turnover, as outlined on our site.

1. Tell us about it

Share the project, the location and your timing, whether it is a concept or ready to hire a project captain.

2. Pre-design

We review requirements, scopes of work and site needs, then prepare schedules and budget cost estimates.

3. Design phase

Value engineering options, constructability and code review, updated estimates and approvals.

4. Build and turnover

Turnkey construction with strong safety and quality controls, then project turnover.
